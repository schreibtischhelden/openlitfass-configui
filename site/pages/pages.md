# Pages

Pages of the instance written in Markdown, such as the imprint or the
privacy policy. Save a page as `site/pages/<name>.md` in the instance
directory; openLitfass shows it at `seite.html?page=<name>`, with the header
and footer of the calendar. The preview next to the text uses the same
renderer, so the page looks the same, apart from the instance's colours.
